Remote Sessions

Specialized Online Workshops for Seniors

Not everyone can make it to a library branch. Our online workshops bring the same patient, one-on-one teaching style to seniors and their families over video call — wherever they are.

Built for Seniors' Unique Needs

Online learning can feel intimidating if the tools themselves are the barrier. We design every remote session around that reality — slower pacing, plain-language instructions, and a volunteer who stays on the call until the participant feels confident, not just until the clock runs out.

Sessions run over Zoom or Google Meet with live screen-sharing, so participants see exactly what to tap or click in real time, and can ask questions the moment something is unclear.

A family caregiver is always welcome to join the call — many of our best sessions happen with a grandchild or adult child sitting alongside, reinforcing what's learned after we hang up.

How a Session Works
  • 1
    We match you with a volunteerBased on the topic you need help with and your schedule.
  • 2
    A quick test callWe confirm your camera, mic, and screen-share work before the real session — no surprises.
  • 3
    Live, one-on-one teachingWe go step by step, repeating and slowing down as often as needed.
  • 4
    A written recapYou get an emailed, large-print cheat sheet of exactly what we covered.
  • 5
    Free follow-upStuck later? Message us and we'll get back on a call at no cost.

Topics We Cover

Video calling family, texting and photos, avoiding scam calls and phishing emails, social media privacy, and setting up new devices.

No Tech Required Beforehand

If you can answer a video call, we can teach you the rest — we walk you through downloading anything needed together.

Completely Free

Like all THHYE programs, online sessions are free, with no obligation and no sales pitch.
