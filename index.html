Two groups, one goal: nobody gets left behind by technology.

Kids are growing up with AI. Many grandparents are still getting used to video calls. We teach both, patiently and in plain language.

For kids and teens

Understand AI before it understands you.

Interactive sessions where students talk, guess, and try things, instead of just listening.

  • What AI is and how it worksWhere AI already shows up, from YouTube to homework help.
  • Using AI responsiblyAI can be wrong. We practice checking answers and thinking for yourself.
  • Building games with AIHands-on projects that turn curiosity into something you made.
  • Digital citizenshipBeing kind, careful, and safe online.

For older adults and families

Your phone, your family, your safety.

One-on-one and small-group help, as slow as you need, with no question too small.

  • Smartphone basicsCalls, texts, apps, and settings, on iPhone or Android.
  • Video callingFaceTime, Zoom, and Google Meet, step by step.
  • Spotting scamsPhishing texts, fake calls, and the warning signs to watch for.
  • Social mediaSharing photos and staying private on Facebook and Instagram.

Every workshop is built for the people coming to it.

A room of fifth graders and a room of grandparents need different lessons. So we don't run one script. Tell us about your group and we'll design the session around them, free.

Plan a workshop with us
  1. Step 1

    Tell us who's coming

    Ages, how comfortable they are with tech, the language they're most at home in, and what they want to learn.

  2. Step 2

    We design the lesson

    Our student team builds slides, activities, and examples around your group, not a generic deck.

    • AI for middle schoolers
    • Tech Made Simple for families
    • Internet safety for seniors
  3. Step 3

    We teach it, in person or online

    At your event, your library, or over video call. Volunteers stay until questions are answered.
