Community Partners

Our Community Partners

THHYE is proud to partner with organizations across Central Ohio to expand free technology education through youth-led community service.

GMOCO

Through this partnership, THHYE works alongside GMOCO's YUVA Connect team to bring practical, welcoming technology workshops to more community members.

YUVA Connect's focus on youth engagement aligns closely with THHYE's mission: students helping others build confidence with everyday digital tools.

Together, we can reach more families, support more learners, and create more opportunities for young volunteers to serve with purpose.

Columbus Maharashtra Mandal

THHYE partners with the Columbus Maharashtra Mandal (CMM) to bring free technology workshops directly to its community, hosted at CMM events and gatherings across Central Ohio.

CMM's mission to preserve culture and bring families together gives THHYE volunteers a welcoming space to teach digital skills to members of all ages, from kids learning new tools to seniors gaining confidence online.

This partnership has already brought hands-on sessions like "Tech Made Simple" to CMM families, with more workshops planned together.
